I'm a Research Scientist at [Persona Identities](https://withpersona.com), where I build machine-learning systems to fight identity fraud. Before joining industry, I spent a decade as a researcher at [UCLA](https://www.atmos.ucla.edu/), studying the physics of Earth's radiation belts and magnetosphere using spacecraft data and large-scale data-driven models.

My research sits at the intersection of **machine learning, plasma physics, and AI for real-world systems**. I have led NASA- and NSF-funded projects developing neural-network models of the near-Earth space environment, and I have published in journals such as _Nature Communications_, _Geophysical Research Letters_, and _JGR: Space Physics_. More recently, I've been working on layout-aware representation learning for open-set fraud detection (CVPR/AI4RWC 2026).

I earned my Ph.D. in Space Physics from [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/) in 2015, with a visiting year at UCLA under Prof. Richard Thorne, and a B.S. in Applied Physics from [Xi'an Jiaotong University](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/).

Outside of research, I serve on the boards of several Chinese-American scientific and alumni organizations, chairing scholarship programs that support students in STEM.
